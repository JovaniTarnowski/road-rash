count, `+4` boxes). Boxes are `{lo, z_lo, hi, z_hi}` relative to the slot
lateral (×3 for `|lateral| > 0x3ff` single-box non-0x4000 slices); a hit runs
`0x00437660` (static hazard) then `0x004049c0`.
