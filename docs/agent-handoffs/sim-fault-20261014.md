# Handoff: SIM-capture fault (October 14, 2026) — blocks plan 10 Task 5's next experiment

## Symptom (deterministic)

Every `RR_ORACLE_SIM=1` capture dies after exactly 2 dumped ticks (frames 0–1,
ticks 1–2), on every course/drive/solo combination tried, with and without
pixels/input/record. Two exit modes: quick clean exit (`FRAMES=60`: the 60-frame
quit fires during the countdown while only 2 ticks stepped) or page fault
`read 000001FE at 7BF63CC2 (ntdll)` + winedbg hang (larger `FRAMES`).
Non-SIM captures (pixels/scene/drive) are healthy. Last working SIM capture:
`sim-napa-solo.tsv`, Oct 11.

## Ruled out (evidence)

- Proxy changes since Oct 11: rebuilt the TRUE pre-hook stock proxy
  (`f798922^`, verified by hash change) — same 2-frame fault. Also ruled out
  individually by temporary bisect (all reverted): seed write, `sim_freeze_others`,
  `dump_sim_tick` (fault persists with `NODUMP`), `hook_dash`.
- Stale Wine processes/winedbg pileups: cleaned repeatedly; fault reproduces
  with an empty process table.
- Claims-registry deadlocks: cleared dead PIDs; unrelated.
- `readable()` is VirtualQuery-based and sound; the 2 dumped frames are complete.
- The installed DLL matches the built source (checked twice).

## Prime suspect (unproven): stale wall-clock timer firing mid-setup

- The fault lands ~4–8 s after race start, always between dump 1 and step 2's
  entry marker (instrumented run: `step 1 dumped`, never `step 2 enter`).
- Session 22 fixed a credits-timer fault for long captures; `hook_menu_enter`
  avoids entering credits, but a 1.5 s re-arm timer (`0x00424230`, seen in the
  frontend session) firing into half-built race surfaces fits the timing and
  the ntdll null-pointer signature.
- Why SIM-only? Unknown — possibly timing (SIM dumps stretch wall-clock), not logic.
